# Cases: Guided Business Investigations

The controller, sales coordinator, operations planner, and design lead need explanations they can act on. These eight cases take you from their question to a reproducible analysis and a supported recommendation.

Each case contains a fully worked investigation in **Excel and SQL**, several explanatory figures, session stopping points, and an independent challenge. Choose either tool to address the shared business question and expected results. Keep a record of your scope, checks, source evidence, and what remains uncertain.

Start with [Resolve a Customer Account Review](o2c-trace-case.md). If you are still learning to filter, join, or summarize records, complete the relevant [Learn and Practice lessons](../../learn-and-practice/index.md) first. The [Excel](../excel-guide.md) and [SQL](../sql-guide.md) setup guides remain available throughout.

Allow **60–90 minutes per session** as a planning estimate. Basic cases have two sessions, intermediate cases three, and advanced cases four. The eight cases total **23 sessions**, or **23–34.5 estimated hours** at that rate, excluding setup, tutorials, and reading. These are planning estimates, not observed completion times. Complete the prerequisites for your selected case without needing to study all eight.

## Check Readiness for the Chosen Module

Start with the [focused module routes](../../learn-and-practice/index.md#choose-a-focused-module). Each case links its prerequisites individually so students in shorter modules can establish the skills its investigation requires. Before a case, show one source trace with an explicit grain, valid key and match-count check, reporting cutoff, independently checked amount, and bounded conclusion. Use the [Lesson 3](../../learn-and-practice/connect-the-records.md#readiness-check-after-lesson-3), [Lesson 6](../../learn-and-practice/find-the-accounting-entries.md#readiness-check-after-lesson-6), and [advanced-case](../../learn-and-practice/turn-records-into-a-conclusion.md#readiness-check-before-an-advanced-case) gates as appropriate to the route.

Use the [blank evidence template](pathname:///teaching/evidence-template.md) and [public method rubric](pathname:///teaching/method-rubric.md) to retain this evidence. The [public method-repair exercise](../../learn-and-practice/index.md#repair-the-method-before-a-challenge) provides method feedback before assessment while keeping independent challenge answers private.

## Keep Evidence, Then Make a Decision

At every stage, predict what you expect, run the analysis, compare the worked checkpoint, and investigate differences. Save the intermediate outputs before continuing. Return to the management request: what can you now explain, which evidence supports it, and what should the team do next?

A complete submission explains the population, calculation, result, source check, limitation, and proposed next action. The worked core shows this method openly. The separate challenge asks you to transfer it to a new population without a public model answer.

After a case, use [Reports and Analytics](../index.md) and the [Query Library](../analysis-tracks.md) as extensions. Their broader measures may use different populations, dates, or definitions, so compare those before comparing totals.
