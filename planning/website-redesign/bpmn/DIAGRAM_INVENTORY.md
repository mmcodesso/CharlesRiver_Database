The six core cycles are accompanied by one architecture model and four focused details. Returns, credit memos and refunds share one correction model because their document and accounting chain belongs together. Cash allocation needs its own scope because receipt and settlement are distinct; service-accrual settlement excludes goods receipts. Manufacturing variance review is explicitly a proposed classroom activity rather than an implemented approval gate.

Source XML contains native editable pools, lanes, BPMN library shapes and connectors. Each SVG embeds the editable diagram and records its source SHA-256. These are visual models, not executable BPMN interchange documents.
